Actinolumen

The Actinids use biological lenses and photocytes to generate and focus light into a laser. Their Morse-code-like communication requires absolute darkness, of which plenty can be found on the eternally dark side of the moon in which they inhabit.

They are called Actinids, from Greek ἀκτίς (actis), which means ray. Their language name originates from actin- and lumen, which is Latin for light.
